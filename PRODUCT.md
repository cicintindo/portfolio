# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS — no build step or runtime dependencies. Served from GitHub Pages. (Established by the existing codebase; no change requested.)

## Users

Primary audience is mixed — the site serves several situations at once (confirmed by the owner):
- Recruiters and employers evaluating Cintia as a candidate for a first internship/role in technology.
- Professor(s) evaluating it as a graded project for the ADS (Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas) course at IFSP.
- Acquaintances and personal network visiting out of interest.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Cintia Kamei, a 2nd-semester ADS student at IFSP. It introduces who she is (education, freelance work on weekends, interests), showcases real projects and technical skills, and offers ways to get in touch. Success means a first internship/opportunity, passing the academic evaluation, and building a consistent professional presence — with no single channel dominant.

## Positioning

An honest, first-person portfolio by a career-changer: her father is a systems analyst, she studied Administration and Environmental Engineering before moving into ADS, and she freelances on weekends. The positioning comes from a genuine, human trajectory in tech rather than a hypothetical product claim.

## Operating Context

- Read on desktop and mobile (mobile web); the visual identity is fully responsive.
- Projects link to live sites and GitHub repositories.
- Contact via a working (front-end only) mailto/Instagram/email form and direct links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: presentation sections (sobre, formação, projetos, competências, experiências, hobbies, viagens, contato), a cinematic entry splash screen, a mobile hamburger menu, an email contact form with client-side validation, and a back-to-top button.
- Constraints: static site (no backend), so form submission is simulated client-side only — there is no actual email sending. Content is Portuguese (pt-BR).

## Brand Commitments

No binding visual or brand constraints were confirmed beyond what the site already implements. The current look (pink/pastel palette, Poppins + DM Mono typefaces, soft-shadow cards, cherry-blossom motif) is the incumbent identity, but the owner declined to make it a permanent requirement.

## Evidence on Hand

Real, verifiable facts already on the site that future work must not contradict:
- Currently in the 2nd semester of ADS at IFSP.
- Former Technical degree in Administration; 2 semesters of Environmental Engineering (courses).
- Works freelance on weekends; her father is a systems analyst.
- Real hobbies (music, her Maltese dog Snow, puzzles) and travel (São Paulo coast, Bahia, Ceará, Paraná).
- Existing projects: "O Mundo do Snow" (live site + repo) and "Cafeteria IFSP" (course freshman project, grade 10).
- Contact email: cintia.kamei@aluno.ifsp.edu.br; Instagram @cicintindo.

## Product Principles

- Honesty over hype: show the real trajectory (career change, freelance, student) rather than invented credentials.
- One site, many visitors: serve recruiters, academic evaluation, and personal network without optimizing for a single persona.
- Progress over polish: reflect that she is early in her career and still learning, not a finished expert.
- Accessibility as a baseline: text must meet readable contrast and font sizes for all audiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text contrast and minimum font sizes must remain readable (WCAG AA). Reduced-motion support is already present and should be preserved.
